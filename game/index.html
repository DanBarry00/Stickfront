Upgrades only affect units trained after the research finishes. Veterans can be refitted at their building for 5 gold per upgrade.

Giants can't be commanded directly: they follow nearby troops, and more troops means more chains and better obedience. Blinders let you give them move and hold orders. Archers hurt and confuse giants. To win, destroy the enemy statue and then kill the giant that bursts out of it.
