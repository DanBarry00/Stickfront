Units and tips

Quartermaster
Your supply man (up to 2), always busy: restocks Gladiators, refits soldiers and miners for free (one at a time), heals, restores gold deposits, brings Architects trap materials and cheers miners on (they work twice as fast). The sign over his head shows what he's doing
Gladiator
Throws one javelin (it hits flyers) and one net (pins a target), then fights with his sword. Every kill makes him tougher (the star badge); he hits harder alone. Garrison him or send a Quartermaster to restock
Necromancer
Raises zombies (the skull badge shows the pack and its souls). Anyone the zombies kill joins the pack while there's room, and every kill grows it. Zombies shrug off arrows, but Healing Pools and Clerics burn them
Architect
Builds trenches, traps and towers and repairs them. Idle, it studies cheaper upgrades: click the book on an upgrade to choose which
Snowballers
A Gladiator full of glory or a Necromancer full of souls only gets stronger: kill them early, or answer with archers and Clerics
Home ground
Troops take less damage the closer they fight to their own castle, and a statue mends itself when no enemy is near. Attacking takes a stronger army than defending
Rally
Fall far behind and your side rallies: units train faster and the castle pays a little extra, so a lost fight isn't a lost game
The Monolith tower
Starts with two neutral archers who shoot at both sides. Bring them down (or the tower), then post up to two of your own archers there
Hope and Fear
Cleric and Warlock upgrades: friends in a Cleric's range attack faster and take less damage; enemies in a Warlock's range the opposite

Upgrades only affect units trained after the research finishes. Veterans can be refitted at their building for 5 gold per upgrade.

Giants can't be commanded directly: they follow nearby troops, and more troops means more chains and better obedience. Blinders let you give them move and hold orders. Archers hurt and confuse giants. To win, destroy the enemy statue and then kill the giant that bursts out of it.
